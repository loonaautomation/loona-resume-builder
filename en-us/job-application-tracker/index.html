Free Job Application Tracker

In short: one simple board for your whole job search - save every vacancy, drag it from Wishlist to Applied, Interview and Offer, and never miss a deadline or a follow-up again. Paid trackers charge $29-$40 a month for this. Here it is completely free, with no sign-up - and your applications never leave your device.
🔒 Private by design: your applications are stored only in this browser, on this device (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, there is no account and no server - we never see your job search. Pair it with the free Resume Builder and the free ATS Resume Checker.

How this tracker works

  1. Save every vacancy. Click Add Application, enter the company and job title, paste the posting link, and set the application deadline so nothing expires quietly.
  2. Move cards as things happen. Drag each card - or use its arrow buttons - through Wishlist → Applied → Interview → Offer. Rejected and Archived columns keep your board honest and tidy.
  3. Follow up on time. Set a follow-up date on any application (a polite nudge a week after applying works well). Overdue follow-ups and deadlines are highlighted at the top of this page and on the cards themselves.
  4. Take your data anywhere. Export CSV to open your board in Excel or Google Sheets, and export JSON as a full backup you can import again on any device. No account, no lock-in - it is your data.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this job application tracker really free?

Yes, completely free, with no limits. Well-known trackers such as Teal+ (about $29/month) and Huntr Pro (about $40/month) charge a subscription for a board like this. Loona's tracker runs entirely in your browser, so there is no server to pay for - and nothing to charge you for.

Where is my application data stored?

Only on your device, in your browser's local storage. It is never uploaded, synced or seen by us, and there is no sign-up. If you clear your browser data the board is cleared too - so export a JSON backup now and then if the search matters to you.

How do the follow-up reminders work?

Give any application a follow-up date. When that date arrives - or passes - the application is listed in a reminder bar at the top of this page and its card is highlighted, until you move it forward or pick a new date. Deadlines get the same treatment: overdue in red, due within three days in amber.

Can I open my tracker in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Export CSV downloads a file with one row per application - company, role, stage, link, deadline, follow-up date and notes - which opens directly in Excel, Google Sheets or LibreOffice. Export JSON saves a complete backup, and Import JSON restores it here, on this or another device.
