What recruiters look for in a lawyer resume

  • Lead with bar admission, practice area and case load — law firms screen on jurisdiction and speciality first.
  • Quantify outcomes without breaking privilege: cases managed, motions won, contract values.
  • Keep the format conservative: Classic template, one page for juniors, two for seniors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a lawyer resume be one page?

One page for juniors and clerks; two pages once you have years of cases and matters to show.

Do I name my clients?

No — never name clients or share privileged details. Describe the matter type and the outcome.

Where does the bar admission go?

Right under your name or in the education section: 'Admitted to the Illinois Bar, 2019'. Recruiters look for it immediately.
