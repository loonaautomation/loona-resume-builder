What recruiters look for in a project manager resume

  • Lead with delivery: projects completed, value managed, on-time rate. That is the whole job in three numbers.
  • Put PMP/CSM and your methods (Agile, Waterfall) near the top — they are the first screen.
  • Show stakeholder skill: executives, clients, vendors and teams all in one bullet each.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should a project manager list every project?

No — list the 3-5 biggest or most relevant, with value and outcome. A full project list belongs in an interview, not on the resume.

Is PMP worth putting at the top?

Yes — put 'PMP' after your name. It is the most searched project-management keyword.

One page or two?

Two pages is normal for project managers with 7+ years and several projects to show.
