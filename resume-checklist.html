Why these twenty checks?

Most applications are first read by software (an applicant tracking system, or ATS) that scans for structure and keywords, and then by a recruiter who gives the page about ten seconds. This checklist covers both readings: the ATS group keeps your file machine-readable, the content group makes your ten seconds count, and the final group catches the small mistakes - a dead email address, a file named resume-final-v3.pdf - that quietly end applications. Work top to bottom, fix each item in the builder, and tick it off. When the bar reaches 100%, the application is ready to send. New to this? Read the free resume guide first.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an ATS and why does it matter?

An applicant tracking system is the software employers use to store and filter applications. If it cannot read your file - because the text is inside images, or headings are unusual - a human may never see it. The ATS group on this page keeps your resume readable to both software and people.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, in your browser's local storage on this device (key: loona_resume_checklist_v1). Nothing is uploaded. Use Reset Checklist to start over for a new application.

Do I need every single tick before applying?

Aim for all twenty - each one exists because it has ended real applications. If one genuinely does not apply to you (for example, you have no LinkedIn), tick it once you have made that decision deliberately, not by accident.
