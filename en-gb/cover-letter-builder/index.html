How to write a cover letter that works

  1. Name the job. The subject line and first sentence must say exactly which position you want - recruiters sort applications by it.
  2. Answer the advertisement. The middle paragraph is not your life story: it is 2-3 requirements from the job ad, each answered with a real, measurable result.
  3. Keep it to one page. Three short paragraphs beat a long letter every time. End by asking for the interview.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this cover letter builder really free, including the PDF?

Yes. Like the resume builder, there is no server and no checkout: the letter is built in your browser and the PDF is created by your browser's print function. Free means free.

Where is my letter saved while I work?

Only in your browser's local storage on this device (key: loona_cover_letter_v1). Nothing is uploaded. Press Clear Everything to delete it permanently.

Should my cover letter match my resume?

Yes - use the same name, title and contact details, and the same professional tone. This builder uses the Classic letter layout that sits naturally beside the Classic resume template.

What if I do not know the hiring manager's name?

Leave the field blank and the letter opens with "Dear Hiring Manager," - completely acceptable. Never invent a name.
