How It Works

  1. Fill in the form. Contact details, a short summary, your jobs and education (add as many as you need), skills and languages.
  2. Pick a template. Choose from 8 templates - Classic, Modern, Minimal, ATS, Creative, Professional, Executive or Compact - switch freely and watch the preview change instantly.
  3. Print or save as PDF. One click opens your browser's print dialog. Choose "Save as PDF" for a file, or print straight to paper - a perfect A4 page either way.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this resume builder really free - including the download?

Yes, completely. Most "free" builders let you type for free and then charge at the download button. Loona has no download step to charge for: your resume is built in your browser and saved as a PDF by your browser's own print function. Free means free - building, both templates, and the PDF.

Do I need to create an account or sign up?

No. There is no account system at all. Open the builder, type, and download. Your work is saved automatically in your own browser so you can come back to it on the same device.

Is there a watermark on the PDF?

Never. The resume you download is exactly the clean A4 page you see in the preview - no logos, no stamps, no "made with" line.

Where does my resume data go?

Nowhere. Everything runs 100% in your browser. Your details are stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device, and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. See the Privacy Policy for the full explanation.

Which template should I choose?

Classic is the safe choice for traditional fields - banking, government, education, law - and prints beautifully in black and white. Modern suits marketing, tech, design and startups, with a coloured header band and a side panel for skills. For applications through hiring software, the dedicated ATS template is the safest choice of all - see the resume templates guide for which template fits your field.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The builder works in any modern mobile browser. The form is designed for small screens, and the A4 preview scrolls sideways on a phone so you always see the true final layout. For printing, a computer gives you the most control.
