What a complete marriage biodata includes

  • Personal details: full name, date and place of birth, height, and the family's language background.
  • Horoscope details (where families ask for them): rashi, nakshatra and gotra - optional here, and the whole section hides if you leave it blank.
  • Education and occupation: the highest qualification, current work, and income where the family is comfortable sharing it.
  • Family details: parents' names and occupations, and siblings with their marital status.
  • Contact details: a family address and a phone number a proposal can be answered on.

Every field marked optional can be left empty - empty fields and empty sections simply do not appear on the printed biodata.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this biodata builder free, including the PDF?

Yes, completely - building, both styles and the PDF download are free, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Where is the biodata saved while I work?

Only in your browser's local storage on this device (key: loona_biodata_v1). Biodata is personal family information, and it is never uploaded anywhere. Press Clear Everything to delete it permanently.

What is the difference between Simple and Decorative?

Simple is a clean, modern layout suited to sharing as a PDF or WhatsApp file. Decorative adds a traditional double gold border and a formal serif heading, suited to printing and handing over in person. Your details move between the two instantly.

Can I leave out religion, caste or horoscope details?

Yes. Those fields are optional by design - many families include them, many do not. Anything left blank is left off the printed biodata entirely.
